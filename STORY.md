# NEON ECHO — The Complete Story

> *"If we ever get lost, climb to the top of the world. I'll find you there."*
> — Aria Vance, on the rooftops of Lowtown

A 2D cyberpunk story told in **five continuous episodes**. Each episode ends on a
sharp cliffhanger, and the next episode opens exactly where the last one cut to
black. You play as **Kade Vance**, a courier searching the neon-drowned sprawl of
**Nyx City** for his missing sister **Aria**. Between the chases and the gunfire,
the story keeps falling back into the past — to the days when the two of them had
nothing but each other, and that was enough. In those flashbacks you play as
**Aria**.

---

## Cast

| Name | Role | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Kade Vance** | Protagonist (present day) | 24. A street courier and rooftop runner. Tough, loyal, running on fumes and hope. Playable in every present-day chapter. |
| **Aria Vance** | Deuteragonist (flashbacks) | 20. Kade's younger sister. A gifted coder who goes by the handle **"Echo."** Playable in every flashback. |
| **Echo** | Mysterious informant | An encrypted voice that feeds Kade leads. *Echo is Aria* — a truth Kade does not learn until Episode 3. |
| **Pix** | Courier kid | A jittery teenage runner in Lowtown who carries Echo's data-shard. |
| **Vex Mercer** | Antagonist | Lead enforcer of **The Hollow**, the syndicate arm of megacorp **Halcyon Dynamics**. Cold, theatrical, patient. |
| **Halcyon Dynamics** | The corporation | Owns the power, the water, and the law in Nyx City. **The Hollow** is its knife in the dark. |

## The City — Nyx City, "the Veil"

A vertical megacity drowning in its own neon. Rain never really stops; it just
changes color under the holograms. Five districts frame the five episodes:

1. **Lowtown** — the slum rooftops where Kade and Aria grew up.
2. **The Skyway** — an elevated neon highway strangling the upper city.
3. **The Sunken District** — the old lower city, flooded after Halcyon dammed the
   reservoir. Drowned streets, a half-submerged cathedral.
4. **The Steel Veins** — the industrial arteries: refineries, rail, pipework.
5. **The Spire** — Halcyon Dynamics' headquarters, the tallest structure in the
   Veil. "The top of the world."

## Backstory

Kade and Aria Vance lost their parents in a "transit accident" on the Skyway when
Kade was sixteen and Aria was twelve. They raised each other in a Lowtown
walk-up: Kade ran packages across the rooftops, Aria taught herself to code on a
salvaged deck. Four months before the game begins, Aria stumbled onto something
inside Halcyon's network — proof that the accident that killed their parents was
no accident at all. The next morning she was gone. No note. No body. Just a cold
mug of tea and a deck wiped clean.

Kade never stopped looking. Then, one rainy night, a message arrives — signed
**ECHO**.

# EPISODE 1 — "Signal in the Rain"
**District:** Lowtown rooftops · **Mechanics:** Running, Parkour · **You play:** Kade, then young Aria (flashback)

### Intro
Rain hammers the tin roofs of Lowtown. Kade's cracked wrist-deck lights up with a
message from an address he's never seen, signed **ECHO**:

> **ECHO:** *She's alive, Kade. The courier on Rivet Row is carrying proof. Catch him before The Hollow does. RUN.*

Four months of silence, and now this. Kade doesn't think. He runs.

### Gameplay — Present (Kade)
A rooftop chase across Lowtown. Sprint, vault, wall-run and leap the gaps between
buildings to catch **Pix**, the courier, before Halcyon's people do. Neon signs
buzz past; the rain makes every ledge a gamble.

### Flashback — "Tag, You're It" (young Aria)
The screen washes out to warm magenta. *Six years ago.* Kade and a twelve-year-old
Aria race across these same rooftops at dusk, playing tag, laughing. You play as
**Aria**, chasing the silhouette of her brother across the skyline — pure, weightless
joy. She catches him at the edge and they sit, legs dangling over Lowtown, and
make a promise:

> **Young Aria:** *If we ever get lost — really lost — climb to the top of the world. The Spire. I'll find you there. Pinky promise.*
> **Young Kade:** *That's the dumbest plan I ever heard.*
> **Young Aria:** *It's a great plan. Pinky. Promise.*

### Cliffhanger
Back in the present, Kade corners Pix on a water tower. Pix is terrified —
he presses a data-shard into Kade's hand:

> **Pix:** *I never saw your face, okay? She's in the Sunken District. The Hollow's got her at the Drowned Cathedral. Echo said you'd—*

A red targeting laser blooms on Pix's chest. A Halcyon **drone-sniper** on a far
rooftop fires once. Pix drops. The laser swings up and finds **Kade**. Alarms
howl across Lowtown. Kade shoves the shard into his pocket and dives off the tower
into the rain as the rooftop behind him erupts.

**Cut to black.** *Get to the Sunken District. Before they do.*

# EPISODE 2 — "Neon Highway"
**District:** The Skyway · **Mechanics:** Driving · **You play:** Kade, then teen Aria (flashback)

### Intro
Kade hits the street still running, drone-spotlights raking the alleys behind him.
The only road to the Sunken District is the **Skyway** — and the only way to beat
The Hollow there is to take it at full speed. He jacks an abandoned courier car at
the on-ramp. The deck crackles:

> **ECHO:** *They've made you. Skyway's the only way down to the Sunken District and they're closing the ramps. Drive. Don't stop for anything.*

### Gameplay — Present (Kade)
A high-speed neon car chase down the Skyway. Weave through traffic, dodge
barricades and Hollow interceptors, keep the speed up as the road buckles and
the city smears into light.

### Flashback — "Joyride" (teen Aria)
*Three years ago.* A warm memory blooms: Kade and a teenage Aria "borrow" a
beat-up car and take the empty old Skyway at midnight, windows down, the whole
glittering city laid out below. You play as **Aria** at the wheel, weaving through
light, both of them laughing themselves stupid.

> **Teen Aria:** *Tell me this isn't the best view in the whole Veil.*
> **Teen Kade:** *Eyes on the road, you maniac!*
> **Teen Aria:** *I AM watching the road. Mostly.*

### Cliffhanger
The present-day Skyway ends in a wall: The Hollow has dropped a barricade across
the ramp down to the Sunken District. Kade floors it and crashes *through* — the
car sails off the broken ramp and plunges into the black flood-water of the lower
city. As it sinks, his shard finally decrypts on the dash: a **photograph**. Aria,
gaunt but **alive**, timestamped *yesterday*. Below it, a Hollow manifest:

> *SUBJECT "ECHO" — DROWNED CATHEDRAL — PROCESSING IN 48:00:00.*

Water floods the cabin. Kade kicks out the windshield and swims up into the dark.

**Cut to black.** *She has a day. Maybe less.*

# EPISODE 3 — "The Drowned District"
**District:** The Sunken District · **Mechanics:** Swimming, Climbing · **You play:** Kade, then young Aria (flashback)

### Intro
Kade surfaces in a drowned street, neon signs flickering underwater like dying
fish. The **Drowned Cathedral** rises out of the flood ahead — The Hollow's
processing site. The deck, half-shorted, sputters one last line from **ECHO**
before going dark:

> **ECHO:** *Almost here. The lab's below the waterline — swim the nave, climb the bell-rigging to the cells. Hurry. They moved the clock up.*

### Gameplay — Present (Kade)
Swim through submerged streets and the flooded nave of the cathedral — manage your
breath, avoid drowned hazards and Hollow divers — then **climb** the bell-rigging
and scaffolding up out of the water to reach the holding cells.

### Flashback — "Lucky Coin" (young Aria)
*Years ago, before the dam.* The reservoir is clean and blue. Aria, the better
swimmer, is teaching a nervous young Kade to dive. You play as **Aria**, diving for
a "lucky coin" she throws into the deep, surfacing with it grinning.

> **Young Aria:** *See? The water's not scary. It just wants you to trust it.*
> **Young Kade:** *Easy for you. You're part fish.*
> **Young Aria:** *Keep the coin. For luck. Don't lose it, dummy.*

### Cliffhanger
Kade reaches the cells. Aria's cell door hangs open. **Empty.** Inside: her jacket,
folded neatly, and a recorder still blinking. He plays it — and for the first time
hears *her* voice, not "Echo's," because they are the same voice:

> **Aria (recording):** *Kade. If you're hearing this, you got my messages. I'm "Echo." It was always me. I didn't run from you — I ran to keep them away from you. I found what really happened to Mom and Dad. Halcyon did it, and I can prove it. I broke out. I'm going to the Spire to upload everything and burn The Hollow to the ground. If I don't make it — finish it for me. I lo—*

The recording cuts to static, then a single gunshot, then nothing. Kade stares at
the jacket. Then he pockets the recorder and turns toward the Spire.

**Cut to black.** *Echo was you. It was always you.*

# EPISODE 4 — "Steel Veins"
**District:** The Steel Veins · **Mechanics:** Riding (motorcycle), Shooting · **You play:** Kade, then Aria (flashback — the night she vanished)

### Intro
The only fast road from the Sunken District to the Spire runs through the **Steel
Veins** — Halcyon's industrial spine, now crawling with Hollow riders hunting the
fugitive "Echo." Kade hot-wires a courier motorcycle.

> **Kade:** *Hold on, Aria. For once, let me be the one who finds you.*

### Gameplay — Present (Kade)
A running motorcycle battle through the refineries and rail-yards. Ride fast,
weave through the pipework, and **shoot** Hollow riders and gun-drones off your tail
while keeping the bike upright.

### Flashback — "The Night" (Aria)
*Four months ago. The night everything broke.* You play as **Aria**, moving quietly
through the dark Lowtown apartment after the discovery that will take her away.
This memory starts happy — the two of them home, safe — and turns to the moment of
separation. She finds the proof on her deck, understands what it means, and makes
the hardest choice: leave before they trace it to Kade. She slips through the
apartment one last time, leaving without waking him, pausing at his door.

> **Aria (whisper):** *I'm sorry. I have to be the one who carries this. If they find it on me, they leave you alone. Climb to the top of the world, big brother. I'll find you there.*

### Cliffhanger
Kade reaches the foot of the Spire — and rides straight into a trap. Floodlights.
**Vex Mercer** stands waiting, calm in the rain, Kade's bike skidding out beneath
him.

> **Vex:** *Kade Vance. The devoted brother. We let you live this long because you make excellent bait. "Echo" is upstairs right now, walking into my building of her own free will to play hero. She'll reach the data-core in minutes. And then we'll have you both.*

Vex's riders close in. Kade looks up the impossible height of the Spire — *the top
of the world* — kicks the downed bike into a guard, and breaks for the doors.

**Cut to black.** *She kept the promise. Now climb.*

---

# EPISODE 5 — "The Spire"
**District:** The Spire · **Mechanics:** Climbing, Parkour, Shooting · **You play:** Kade (with brief flashback)

### Intro
Inside the Spire, every floor is a war. Aria is somewhere above, racing the same
clock, trying to reach the data-core at the summit before The Hollow stops her.
Kade starts climbing.

> **ECHO / Aria (on the deck):** *Kade?? You're IN the building? You absolute — okay. OKAY. Get to the top. The core's at the summit. We finish this together or not at all.*
> **Kade:** *Took the words right out of my mouth. Don't you dare move.*

### Gameplay — Present (Kade)
The vertical finale. **Climb** the Spire's exterior rigging and interior shafts,
**parkour** across collapsing floors, and **shoot** through Hollow security as you
ascend toward the summit. The city falls away beneath you into a sea of light.

### Flashback — "Pinky Promise" (brief, intercut)
As Kade climbs, the old rooftop promise flickers through — the two kids, legs over
the edge of Lowtown, pinky-swearing to meet *at the top of the world.* The promise
and the climb become the same motion.

### Finale
Kade reaches the summit data-core as dawn cracks the horizon — and there she is.
**Aria.** Thinner, scarred, alive. For a moment neither of them moves.

> **Aria:** *...You actually climbed to the top of the world.*
> **Kade:** *You said you'd find me here. Figured I'd save you the walk.*

Vex makes his last play; together — brother and sister, the two playable hearts of
this story finally in the same frame — they hold him off long enough to slam the
upload home. Halcyon's crimes flood every screen in Nyx City at once. The Hollow's
grip breaks. As the first real sunrise in years burns the neon pale, Kade and Aria
stand at the top of the world, side by side.

> **Aria:** *Mom and Dad. Everyone. They'll know now.*
> **Kade:** *Yeah. They will.* *(beat)* *Don't ever disappear on me again.*
> **Aria:** *Deal. Pinky promise.*

**THE END.**

Every episode flows directly out of the previous one's cliffhanger. The sister,
Aria, is playable **only** in the flashbacks; the brother, Kade, is the
present-day protagonist throughout. He finds her — alive — at the very end of
Episode 5.
